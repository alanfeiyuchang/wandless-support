Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Wandless does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The game contains no networking code, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not require an account. This policy covers Wandless on Apple Vision Pro and on Meta Quest. On Apple Vision Pro, the only thing that ever leaves your device is optional and handled by Apple: Game Center scores and achievements, described below. On Meta Quest, nothing leaves your headset.

Hand tracking

Every spell in Wandless is a hand gesture. With your permission, the game reads the positions of your hands and fingers to recognise those gestures: through Apple's ARKit on Apple Vision Pro, and through the headset's own hand tracking on Meta Quest. This happens on your headset, frame by frame, in memory. Hand data is never transmitted.

On Apple Vision Pro, hand data is never recorded or stored either. On Meta Quest, the game keeps a short diagnostic log in its own storage on the headset. While you make the gesture that summons the book, the log can note a few measurements of your hands, such as how far each finger is bent. The log is replaced every time the game starts, and it never leaves the headset.

Your surroundings

With your permission, the game reads the shape of your room — walls, floor and the surfaces of furniture. On Apple Vision Pro it reads them through ARKit, together with an estimate of the room's lighting; on Meta Quest it reads the room you set up in Space Setup. That is what lets creatures come out of your real walls and stand on your real floor. The room data is used on the device while you play and is never stored or transmitted. On Meta Quest, the passthrough view of your room is drawn by the headset itself; Wandless does not receive camera images.

Where you look

Aiming follows the direction of your head and your outstretched arm. visionOS does not give apps information about where your eyes are looking, and Wandless never receives it. On Meta Quest, aiming follows the direction of your head, and Wandless does not use eye tracking.

Game progress

Your progress — how far you have descended, what you have carved into the book, your best depth, and your language — is saved in the game's own private storage on your device. Apart from the Game Center scores and achievements below, Wandless does not upload it anywhere. If you use iCloud Backup, it may be included in your device's backup, which Apple manages.

Game Center (Apple Vision Pro)

Wandless's own code makes no network connections. The one exception is Game Center, which is optional and provided by Apple: if you are signed in, your scores (deepest depth, fastest clear and best score) and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center to appear on leaderboards and your profile. Apple handles this under your Game Center account and Apple's Privacy Policy; Wandless does not receive your Game Center identity. You can turn Game Center off in Settings.

Diagnostics

The game sends no crash reports or usage data. If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with me; that is Apple's setting, and you can turn it off at any time. On Meta Quest, Meta may show me crash reports and usage statistics for the app in its developer dashboard; Meta collects these under its own privacy policy, and Wandless's own code sends nothing. If you contact me by email, only what you choose to write is shared.

Children

Wandless collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores lives inside the app. Deleting Wandless from your device removes all of it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 16 日

Wandless 不收集、不存储、不传输、也不分享任何个人数据。 游戏里没有任何联网代码，没有统计分析、广告或追踪，也不需要账号。本政策适用于 Apple Vision Pro 和 Meta Quest 上的 Wandless。在 Apple Vision Pro 上，唯一会离开你设备的，是由 Apple 提供、可选的 Game Center 成绩和成就，见下文。在 Meta Quest 上，没有任何数据会离开你的头显。

手部追踪

游戏里的每个法术都是一个手势。经你允许后，游戏读取双手和手指的位置来识别手势：在 Apple Vision Pro 上通过 Apple 的 ARKit，在 Meta Quest 上通过头显自带的手部追踪。这些都在你的头显上逐帧在内存中完成， 手部数据不会被传输。

在 Apple Vision Pro 上，手部数据也不会被记录或存储。在 Meta Quest 上，游戏会在头显上自己的存储空间里保留一份简短的诊断日志： 你做召唤魔典的手势时，日志可能会记下几项手部数值，比如每根手指弯曲的程度。这份日志在每次启动游戏时都会被替换， 也不会离开头显。

周围环境

经你允许后，游戏读取房间的形状（墙、地面和家具表面）：在 Apple Vision Pro 上通过 ARKit， 同时读取房间的光照估计；在 Meta Quest 上读取你在「空间设置」（Space Setup）中设置的房间。 这让怪物能从你真实的墙里出来、站在真实的地面上。这些数据只在游玩时于设备上使用，不会被存储或传输。 在 Meta Quest 上，你看到的房间透视画面由头显自己绘制，Wandless 不会获得摄像头画面。

你看向哪里

瞄准依据的是头部朝向和你伸出的手臂。visionOS 不向任何 app 提供眼睛注视的位置，Wandless 也从未获得这类信息。 在 Meta Quest 上，瞄准依据的是头部朝向，Wandless 不使用眼动追踪。

游戏进度

你的进度（下潜到哪一层、魔典上刻下的内容、最深纪录和语言）保存在设备上游戏自己的私有存储里， 除下面的 Game Center 成绩和成就外，Wandless 不会把它上传到任何地方。如果你开启了 iCloud 云备份， 它可能包含在由 Apple 管理的设备备份中。

Game Center（Apple Vision Pro）

游戏自己的代码不发起任何网络连接。唯一例外是 Apple 提供的、可选的 Game Center：如果你已登录， 你的成绩（最深深度、最快通关、最高分）和获得的成就会发送到 Apple 的 Game Center，显示在排行榜和你的个人资料中。 这由 Apple 按你的 Game Center 账户和 Apple 隐私政策处理，Wandless 不会获得你的 Game Center 身份。 你可以随时在「设置」中关闭 Game Center。

诊断信息

游戏不发送崩溃报告或使用数据。如果你在设备设置中选择了与 app 开发者共享分析数据， Apple 可能会把匿名的崩溃报告提供给我；这是 Apple 的设置，你可以随时关闭。 在 Meta Quest 上，Meta 可能会在开发者后台向我展示这个 app 的崩溃报告和使用统计； 这些由 Meta 按其隐私政策收集，Wandless 自己的代码不发送任何内容。 如果你通过邮件联系我，只会分享你自己写下的内容。

儿童

Wandless 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

删除数据

游戏存储的一切都在 app 内部。从设备上删除 Wandless 即会全部清除。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，更新后的版本会发布在本页，并更新上方的日期。
