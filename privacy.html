Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Wandless does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The game contains no networking code, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not require an account. The only thing that ever leaves your device is optional and handled by Apple: Game Center scores and achievements, described below.

Hand tracking

Every spell in Wandless is a hand gesture. With your permission, the game reads the positions of your hands and fingers through Apple's ARKit to recognise those gestures. This happens on your Apple Vision Pro, frame by frame, in memory. Hand data is never recorded, never stored and never transmitted.

Your surroundings

With your permission, the game reads the shape of your room — walls, floor and the surfaces of furniture — and an estimate of its lighting, also through ARKit. That is what lets creatures come out of your real walls, stand on your real floor and be lit like the room they are in. The room data is used on the device while you play and is never stored or transmitted.

Where you look

Aiming follows the direction of your head and your outstretched arm. visionOS does not give apps information about where your eyes are looking, and Wandless never receives it.

Game progress

Your progress — how far you have descended, what you have carved into the book, your best depth, and your language — is saved in the game's own private storage on your device. Apart from the Game Center scores and achievements below, Wandless does not upload it anywhere. If you use iCloud Backup, it may be included in your device's backup, which Apple manages.

Game Center

Wandless's own code makes no network connections. The one exception is Game Center, which is optional and provided by Apple: if you are signed in, your scores (deepest depth, fastest clear and best score) and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center to appear on leaderboards and your profile. Apple handles this under your Game Center account and Apple's Privacy Policy; Wandless does not receive your Game Center identity. You can turn Game Center off in Settings.

Diagnostics

The game sends no crash reports or usage data. If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with me; that is Apple's setting, and you can turn it off at any time. If you contact me by email, only what you choose to write is shared.

Children

Wandless collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores lives inside the app. Deleting Wandless from your device removes all of it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 8 日

Wandless 不收集、不存储、不传输、也不分享任何个人数据。 游戏里没有任何联网代码，没有统计分析、广告或追踪，也不需要账号。唯一会离开你设备的，是由 Apple 提供、可选的 Game Center 成绩和成就，见下文。

手部追踪

游戏里的每个法术都是一个手势。经你允许后，游戏通过 Apple 的 ARKit 读取双手和手指的位置来识别手势。 这些都在你的 Apple Vision Pro 上逐帧在内存中完成，手部数据不会被记录、存储或传输。

周围环境

经你允许后，游戏通过 ARKit 读取房间的形状（墙、地面和家具表面）以及光照估计， 让怪物能从你真实的墙里出来、站在真实的地面上，并按房间的光线照亮。 这些数据只在游玩时于设备上使用，不会被存储或传输。

你看向哪里

瞄准依据的是头部朝向和你伸出的手臂。visionOS 不向任何 app 提供眼睛注视的位置，Wandless 也从未获得这类信息。

游戏进度

你的进度（下潜到哪一层、魔典上刻下的内容、最深纪录和语言）保存在设备上游戏自己的私有存储里， 除下面的 Game Center 成绩和成就外，Wandless 不会把它上传到任何地方。如果你开启了 iCloud 云备份， 它可能包含在由 Apple 管理的设备备份中。

Game Center

游戏自己的代码不发起任何网络连接。唯一例外是 Apple 提供的、可选的 Game Center：如果你已登录， 你的成绩（最深深度、最快通关、最高分）和获得的成就会发送到 Apple 的 Game Center，显示在排行榜和你的个人资料中。 这由 Apple 按你的 Game Center 账户和 Apple 隐私政策处理，Wandless 不会获得你的 Game Center 身份。 你可以随时在「设置」中关闭 Game Center。

诊断信息

游戏不发送崩溃报告或使用数据。如果你在设备设置中选择了与 app 开发者共享分析数据， Apple 可能会把匿名的崩溃报告提供给我；这是 Apple 的设置，你可以随时关闭。 如果你通过邮件联系我，只会分享你自己写下的内容。

儿童

Wandless 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

删除数据

游戏存储的一切都在 app 内部。从设备上删除 Wandless 即会全部清除。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，更新后的版本会发布在本页，并更新上方的日期。
