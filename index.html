Support

Wandless is a mixed-reality spellcasting game for Apple Vision Pro. Something not working, or not behaving the way you expected? Get in touch.

alanfeiyuchang@gmail.com

Please include your visionOS version and what you were doing — which depth, which spell — it makes most problems much quicker to place.

Play safely

Clear some space around you before you enter the room: you will reach out, turn to face creatures and step aside from their fire. Play in a well-lit room — the headset needs light to see your hands. If your arms get tired, summon the book: the fight waits for you.

Common questions

How do I start?
Allow hand tracking and access to your surroundings, then choose Enter the room. The game begins when you call the grimoire: hold both hands up in front of you with the palms facing away, thumb, index and middle finger straight, ring and little finger curled, hands apart — and hold the pose for about a second. The start card shows it on video.
The book won't come.
Make sure the room is well lit and both hands are in front of you, in view of the headset. If it still isn't recognised, the start card offers Open the grimoire anyway after about twenty seconds.
Why does it need hand tracking and my surroundings?
Every spell is a hand gesture, and creatures come out of your real walls and walk on your real floor. Neither kind of data ever leaves your device — see the privacy policy. If you turned either off, you can allow it again in the Settings app, under Wandless or under Privacy & Security.
How do I pause?
Call the book with the same pose. While it is open, everything in the room stops. Taking the headset off, or opening Control Center, stops the fight and opens the book for you too.
Can I move the book?
Yes. Look at the book and pinch — the usual visionOS look-and-pinch, with your hand away from the book — then move your hand. Pages are turned by touching them, so a pinch made on the pages themselves leaves the book where it is.
Is my progress saved?
Yes. A descent is saved at every gate between depths; if you leave in the middle of a fight, you come back to the start of that depth. What you carve into the book stays with you from one descent to the next.
How do I change the language?
On the start card, before you enter the room. Wandless is in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano and Português (Brasil).
How do I start over from nothing?
Delete Wandless from your device and install it again. Everything it stores lives inside the app.
Can I get a refund?
Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a bug

The most useful report says what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. The depth you were on, the creatures on the field and the spell in your hand usually narrow it down straight away.

安全游玩

进入房间前，请清出身边的空间：游戏里你会伸手、转身面对怪物、侧步躲开它们的火焰。 请在光线充足的房间里玩，头显需要光线才能看清你的手。手臂累了就唤出魔典，战斗会停下来等你。

常见问题

怎么开始？
允许手部追踪和周围环境的访问，然后点「进入这个空间」。唤出魔典游戏就开始了： 双手抬到身前，掌心朝外，拇指、食指、中指伸直，无名指和小指蜷起，双手分开，保持约一秒。 开始卡片上有示范视频。
魔典召不出来。
确认房间光线充足、双手都在身前头显看得到的地方。如果还是识别不出来， 大约二十秒后开始卡片上会出现「直接打开魔典」。
为什么需要手部追踪和周围环境？
每个法术都是手势，怪物会从你真实的墙里出来、走在真实的地面上。这两类数据都不会离开你的设备， 详见隐私政策。如果关掉了，可以在「设置」app 里 Wandless 或「隐私与安全性」下重新允许。
怎么暂停？
用同一个姿势唤出魔典，魔典打开时房间里的一切都会停下。摘下头显或打开控制中心， 战斗也会自动停下并打开魔典。
魔典能移动吗？
能。看着魔典，用 visionOS 常用的「看 + 捏」手势（手离开书，不要捏在书页上），然后移动手即可。 书页是用手指碰来翻的，所以捏在书页上不会移动魔典。
进度会保存吗？
会。每过一层的关口都会存档；如果在战斗中途离开，回来时从这一层的开头继续。 刻在魔典上的内容会一直保留到下一次下潜。
怎么换语言？
在进入房间之前的开始卡片上切换。支持十种语言。
怎么完全重新开始？
删除 Wandless 后重新安装即可，游戏存储的一切都在 app 内部。
能退款吗？
购买通过 Apple 完成，退款也通过 Apple：reportaproblem.apple.com。
